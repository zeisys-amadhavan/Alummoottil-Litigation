## OBJECTIVE — why (non-legal)

**Take control of the Trust and its trustees.** Restore the ₹11,99,85,900 and the heritage property (Meda, Nalukettu, Nelpura) to the beneficiary class; end the capture by D2/D3; secure Anoop's rightful place. Settlement is acceptable only as a route to this — control by consent instead of control by decree.

**Constraints (never breached):**
- **No deposit/security ever** — no O.39 R.10 or s.151 deposit, no rent into court, no bank guarantee/first charge, no revenue-collecting receiver, no arrangement that lets the lease continue because money is protected. Permitted: disclosure of rent received, restraint of receipt/implementation/renewal/disbursement, custody-and-preservation receiver, and post-adjudication restitution.
- **No affirmative reliance on later deeds** (66/IV/2022, 17/IV/2024, 38/IV/2026): impugned documents and evidence against defendants only. Trust terms derive solely from Deed 2/IV/2007.
- **Never telegraph Rule 21** — not in IA 6/2026 papers, notices, submissions or the proposed order. Internally primary; externally invisible until a proved, served, breached order exists.
- Nothing unverified is ever sworn; arithmetic recalculated after every production. Zero voluntary adjournments from 10.08.
- Never seek relief absent from the operative plaint, beyond pecuniary jurisdiction, or against a person unheard.

**Operating principles:** Existence ≠ disclosure (s.119 Ill.(g) BSA — independent of Rule 21) · Reveal ≠ rewind · **Invoices ≠ ledgers**: the demand is contemporaneous source documents — contracts, work orders, measurements, GST invoices, bills, receipts, signed cash vouchers — never "the accounts"; a daybook or later audit cannot replace them · the ₹22-lakh bank-visible payments are the **specimen**: they show vendor transactions occurred, they are **not** compliance and do not by themselves prove invoices existed · "personal expenditure" does not excuse disclosure of documents that entered D1/D2's possession or power or created Trust liabilities/assets/resolutions — but records held **exclusively** by D3 are pursued by third-party summons, never mislabelled as D1/D2's default · ₹11,99,85,900 is the **Trust's own admitted expenditure figure requiring a complete account** — never called "missing" or "stolen" in court · characterization: money minuted, itemized and EMI-scheduled in the Trust's book is trust money.
