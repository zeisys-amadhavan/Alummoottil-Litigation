### Constraints — never breached

- **No deposit/security ever** — no O.39 R.10 or s.151 deposit, no rent into court, no bank guarantee/first charge, no revenue-collecting receiver, no arrangement that lets the lease continue because money is protected. Permitted: disclosure of rent received, restraint of receipt/implementation/renewal/disbursement, custody-and-preservation receiver, and post-adjudication restitution.
- **No affirmative reliance on later deeds** (66/IV/2022, 17/IV/2024, 38/IV/2026): impugned documents and evidence against defendants only. Trust terms derive solely from Deed 2/IV/2007.
- **Never telegraph Rule 21** — not in IA 6/2026 papers, notices, submissions or the proposed order. Internally primary; externally invisible until a proved, served, breached order exists.
- Nothing unverified is ever sworn; arithmetic recalculated after every production.
- **Zero voluntary adjournments.**
- Never seek relief absent from the operative plaint, beyond pecuniary jurisdiction, or against a person unheard.

### Operating principles

**The objective is not to generate more litigation.** Every new IA, amendment, OP, complaint or separate proceeding creates cost, delay, service obligations, jurisdiction questions and additional procedural defences. Use an existing live procedural vehicle whenever it can legally produce the required result. A new proceeding is a tool of necessity, not the default response to resistance or delay.

**Existing vehicle first.** Before filing anything new, read the operative prayers, objections, rejoinders and subsequent developments. Determine whether an existing prayer remains alive and can deliver the required protection. Do not abandon a useful pending application merely because another prayer within it has become stale.

**Delay is itself evidence for escalation, not an invitation to multiply trial-court applications.** Where an existing IA is repeatedly posted without adjudication, the first question is whether supervisory/appellate control can force adjudication of that existing vehicle — not whether another IA should be added to the same queue.

**Prayer-specific analysis.** Mootness, non-joinder, maintainability and prejudice are analysed prayer by prayer. One stale prayer does not automatically extinguish an independent live prayer.

**Record first, law second.** Every litigation recommendation starts with the actual pleading, actual prayer, actual objection and actual hearing history. Generic procedural doctrine never substitutes for the record.

**Existence ≠ disclosure** — s.119 Ill.(g) BSA remains independent of Rule 21.

**Reveal ≠ rewind.** Late selective production does not erase earlier sworn positions or cure absence of the source records demanded.

**Invoices ≠ ledgers.** The demand is contemporaneous source documents — contracts, work orders, measurements, GST invoices, bills, receipts, signed cash vouchers — never merely "the accounts." A daybook or later audit cannot replace them.

The ₹22-lakh bank-visible payments are a **specimen**: they show vendor transactions occurred; they are not compliance and do not by themselves prove invoices existed.

"Personal expenditure" does not excuse disclosure of documents that entered D1/D2's possession or power or created Trust liabilities/assets/resolutions. Records held **exclusively** by D3 are pursued through the appropriate third-party route and are never mislabelled as D1/D2's default.

₹11,99,85,900 is the **Trust's own admitted expenditure figure requiring a complete account** — never called "missing" or "stolen" in court.

Money minuted, itemized and EMI-scheduled in the Trust's own book is characterized as Trust money.

### Litigation-minimization rule

Before opening any new track, ask:

1. Is the desired relief already contained in a live prayer?
2. Is the obstacle substantive, or merely that the court has not decided it?
3. Would a new filing simply enter the same queue?
4. Does the new filing create a new non-joinder, limitation, service, jurisdiction or amendment defence?
5. Can supervisory or appellate relief force adjudication of the existing vehicle instead?
6. Does the new filing strengthen the ultimate objective enough to justify those costs?

If an existing proceeding can legally obtain the result, **press that proceeding rather than duplicate it.**

## RULE 21 ARCHITECTURE — INTERNAL ONLY

Rule 21 remains the central procedural objective but is externally invisible until its foundation exists.

The required chain is:

**clear discovery / inspection order  
→ precise obligation  
→ proved service or knowledge  
→ fixed compliance period  
→ expiry  
→ itemized non-compliance  
→ any final opportunity required by fairness / precedent  
→ continued wilful default  
→ demonstrated prejudice  
→ separate noticed Rule 21 application**

### Do not confuse these stages

Selective pre-order production is **evidence supporting the need for a proper order**. It is not breach of an order that did not yet exist.

A defective first response after the order is **documented non-compliance**, but because Rule 21 is penal the safer architecture remains a clear defect matrix and, where appropriate, a final peremptory opportunity.

Records exclusively outside a defendant's possession/custody/power cannot form that defendant's Rule 21 default.

The final Rule 21 application is made against the actual defaulting defendant based on the actual order and actual breach.

## BOTTOM LINE

The case is not advanced by filing the largest number of applications.

It is advanced by:

**using the strongest existing procedural vehicle  
→ obtaining a clear enforceable order  
→ preserving the subject matter while that occurs  
→ documenting every material default  
→ keeping the affirmative trial case independently provable  
→ opening a new proceeding only when the existing record cannot legally produce the required result.**
