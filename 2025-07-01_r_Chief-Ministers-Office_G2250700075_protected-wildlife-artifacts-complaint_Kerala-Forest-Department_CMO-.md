Subject: Complaint regarding the illegal removal and suspected export of protected
wildlife artifacts from Alummoottil Meda premises – Request for investigation

Respected Sirs,

I write to formally report a serious and cognizable offence involving the illegal removal and
suspected export of protected wildlife artifacts from the premises of Alummoottil Meda, a
family Trust property located in Muttom, Haripad, Alappuzha. As a legal Beneficiary of the
Trust, I consider it my duty to bring this matter to the attention of the competent authorities
for appropriate action under the law.

On 23rd December 2023, during a visit to the Meda, I discovered that two historically
significant heirlooms—a wild bison skull and deer antlers—were missing from their
customary display locations on the walls. These artifacts had remained in the family’s
custodial possession for decades. Despite months of request, the custodians of the Meda have
not disclosed if these heirlooms are still in possession.

To my knowledge, no report or disclosure has been made to the Kerala Forest Department or
the local police regarding the possession, removal, or export of these wildlife items. The
Meda is owned by the Alummoottil Tharavad Trust. Mr. Ramesh Chandran M is the
Managing Trustee, and Mr. Sivadasan M was entrusted with the renovation of the Meda.

In a conversation, Ms. Jiji Ramesh (wife of Ramesh Chandran) boasted that her son, Mr.
Bimal Ramesh Chandran, had personally exported these items to the UAE, thereby
confirming their unauthorized removal. These actions, if left unaddressed, would constitute a
serious breach of fiduciary and custodial responsibility.

I am prepared to furnish any supporting materials, including photographs and witness
statements, upon request. Thank you for your attention to this matter. Kindly acknowledge
receipt of this complaint and advise on the next steps. While I am currently residing in the
United States, I am reachable via, WhatsApp, or email.
