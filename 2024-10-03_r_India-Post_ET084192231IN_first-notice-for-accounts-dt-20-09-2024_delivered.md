>
> As both the son of your brother Radhakrishnan Madhavan Channar, and a beneficiary of the Alummoottil Tharavad Trust, I wish to bring to your attention to some concerns regarding the Trust's management.
>
> On my visit to the Trust property on August 9, 2024, when attempting to access the new building built in the space of the erstwhile Alummoottil Meda, some strangers verbally threatened and physically harmed me, prompting me to call the police for my protection. The following day, on August 10, 2024, a false FIR was filed against me by one Mr. Arun B. Unni. I understand that the strangers involved in this incident were either Trust employees or acting under the instruction of you, Jeeji Ramesh (your wife), and Sivadasan (your elder brother). I have video evidence of these strangers initiating the physical altercation.
>
> Considering these incidents, I respectfully request the following actions be taken by you within fourteen (14) days of your receipt of this letter:
>
> **1. Restoration of Access to Trust Property:**
> Please provide written confirmation that I will have unrestricted access to all Trust properties and a clear process for obtaining keys to the Trust buildings.
>
> **2. Provision of Trust Documents:**
> Kindly supply true copies of the 2007 Trust Deed, the 2022 amendment, the minutes of all Trustee meetings over the past five (5) years, and subsequent amendments made to date.
>
> **3. Audited Accounts:**
> Please provide the audited accounts of the Trust for the fiscal years 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, and the completed months of 2024.
>
> **4. Appointment and Delegation Records:**
> Provide documentation outlining the appointment / delegation, terms, job titles, roles, and responsibilities for the following individuals, along with any other persons working within Trust properties:
> a) Arun B Unni  b) Natarajan  c) Nandakumar  d) Bobby  e) Stalin  f) All other persons
>
> **5. Clarification on False Allegations:**
> I have come across a statement circulated by Sivadasan in WhatsApp groups, falsely claiming I have no rights in the Trust, that I am a constant nuisance, and that I have caused damage to the property. Kindly clarify why these claims are being made on behalf of the Trust.
>
> I trust that you will address my concerns and respond to my requests promptly. In the absence of a reply within fourteen (14) days, I may need to consider alternative steps to safeguard my rights as a beneficiary. Please send your response and future correspondence to me via registered post to the Indian address provided below:
>
> ANOOP RADHAKRISHNAN MADHAVAN
> 12 TV Road, Chetpet,
> Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600031
>
> Thank you for your attention to this matter. I look forward to your response.
