5) The productions also fail to reconcile Defendants’ admitted figures: ₹11,45,85,900 construction + ₹54,00,000 land = ₹11,99,85,900, with ₹1,46,86,000 repayable to D2, his son and contractor (Minutes p. 19, 24.01.2026).

6) Of ₹11,99,85,900, ₹8,76,99,900 is attributed to D3 without supporting calculation. This supports adverse inference against sole ownership (BSA s. 119, Ill. (g)); proven wrongful mingling attracts a charge of the full amount under the Indian Trusts Act (ITA), s. 66.

7) Minutes p. 19 instead lists six contributors: D3 ₹8,76,99,900; Dr. Ravindran ₹1 crore; Dr. Manoj Ravi ₹67 lakh; C.K. Gita ₹54 lakh; Bimal ₹8 lakh; D2 ₹93.86 lakh.

8) Deed 2/IV/2007 requires D2 to keep and present accounts and operate the bank account (cls. 2, 4, 16); ITA ss. 19 and 57 reinforce that duty. Any delegation must satisfy ITA s. 47.

9) On 12.02.2020 the Trust had two Trustees, but Radhakrishnan neither attended nor signed. Absent assent or lawful authority, that resolution cannot bind the Trust or relieve D2 from accounting.

10) D2/D4 personally own adjoining Re.Sy. 219/20, 219/25, 219/26 and 219/4—more land than the Trust—creating severe conflict while ₹11,99,85,900 remains unaccounted. Any advantage gained in that position of conflict is held for the beneficiaries — Indian Trusts Act, ss. 51 and 88.

11) Minutes p. 20 twice calls Plaintiff “beneficiary”. At line 20 it is struck out without countersignature; at line 29 “he may claim” is added by caret. These alterations admit Defendants’ knowledge and warrant adverse inference under BSA s. 119(1) — the Court’s general power to presume, of which Ill. (g) is only one illustration — read with the maxim *omnia praesumuntur contra spoliatorem*: everything is presumed against the party who tampers with a record. The custodian must explain each alteration; fabricating a record for use in a judicial proceeding is separately punishable (BNS ss. 227, 229 — reserved) — against D2’s contrary plea.

12) Even if 17/IV/2024 were valid, cl. 40 requires eleven record categories, eight unproduced; cl. 33(j) required the FY 2025–26 audit by 31.05.2026.
