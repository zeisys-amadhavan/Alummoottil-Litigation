JUDGMENT

This Original Petition is filed seeking the following reliefs :

A. Issue an appropriate writ, order or direction directing the learned Munsiff Court, Haripad, to take up, hear and dispose of Exhibit P6, I.A.No.6/2026 filed under Order XI Rules 12 and 14 of the Code of Civil Procedure, after affording both parties an opportunity of being heard and in accordance with law, within a time limit to be fixed by this Hon'ble Court.

B. Direct the learned Munsiff Court, Haripad, while considering I.A.No.6/2026, to take into account the pleadings, affidavit, rejoinder and order in I.A.No.5/2025, together with the subsequent disclosures and production of Trust records by the respondents, insofar as they are relevant for adjudication of the application under Order XI Rules 12 and 14 CPC.

2. The only relief sought for is an expeditious disposal of I.A.No.6/2026 in O.S.No.214/2025. This Court had called for a report from the learned Munsiff. In obedience to the said order, the learned Munsiff has furnished a report on 13.7.2026 stating that, she requires two months' time to dispose of the I.A.

Taking note of the report, this Original Petition is disposed of directing the Munsiff Court, Haripad, to consider and pass orders on merits in I.A.6/2026, within two months from the date of receipt of a copy of this judgment.
