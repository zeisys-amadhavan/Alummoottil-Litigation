## NOMENCLATURE

An **assertion** is a simple statement about the case or defendant. Each assertion stands on its own and will have several arguments that support it.

An **argument** is a point made to back up an assertion, and many arguments can support one assertion.

A **fact** is a piece of evidence used to build an argument, and many facts can support one argument. The facts can be of the following:

* **Contradicted** — two of their own records cannot both be true
* **False** — a sworn or filed statement disproved by a document
* **Invalid** — the act fails a requirement in the deed or its own terms
* **Unauthorized** — done without the resolution, office, or power relied on
* **Concealed** — existed and was withheld, or produced only when forced
* **Unaccounted** — money or property moved with no supporting record
* **Self-dealing** — a trustee or insider benefits from the act
* **Irreversible** — the harm cannot be undone by money
* **Conceded** — their own record admits the thing they deny
