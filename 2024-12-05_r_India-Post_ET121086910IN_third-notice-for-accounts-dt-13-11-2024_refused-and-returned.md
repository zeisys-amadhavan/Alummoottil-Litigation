## NOTES

- **The refusal sequence is fully documented by a neutral government record:** intimation served on the absent addressee (30.11), fresh delivery attempt (02.12, 16:24:42), refusal two minutes later (02.12, 16:26:52), return journey Kakkanad → Kochi → Chennai → Chetput, receipt back at the sender's own address (05.12). No inference is needed; India Post's own event log states "Item Returned Refused."
- The letter records, from the sender's side, the **exact delivery timestamp of the second notice** (28.10.2024, 16:55:20) — matching ET084195136IN's tracking to the second. The chain closes: notice 1 delivered → notice 2 proves it and is delivered → notice 3 proves that and is **refused**.
- Chronological context: the refusal (02.12.2024) sits between D2's Kakkanad address accepting court-adjacent mail and the filing of O.S. 214/2025 six months later — the plaint's "copies refused despite repeated requests" now has its documentary trilogy.
- "nansy" in the return-delivery event is the recipient at the **sender's** Chetpet address (return leg), not the addressee — do not misread the log.
