# NOTES FOR COUNSEL
## Private complaint under BNSS s.223 — Radhakrishnan Madhavan Channar

*Working notes. Not to be filed. Not part of the complaint.*


**1. Two independent props for dishonesty.** The accused's act was (i) contrary to the registered deed he himself signed in 2007, which fixes the beneficiary class by descent and gives no trustee power to name a sole beneficiary; and (ii) executed during three pending suits, on the precise issue in one of them, five weeks after a co-trustee had sworn the complainant was un-nominated. Neither prop requires proof of the accused's state of mind in 2009. Both should be argued at the s.223 hearing.

**2. The entrustment problem is solved by the accused's own letter of 26.09.2010.** He states there that he took the complainant's money to be advanced to the company *in the complainant's name*, with interest to the complainant, and that a cheque for Dhs 111,130 was drawn in the complainant's name. That is entrustment with dominion, not a debtor-creditor loan, and it makes s.316 a primary count rather than an alternative. Establish what became of that cheque before filing.

**3. Consider leading on the October 2010 transaction.** Request with stated purpose and promise of repayment (02.10.2010), wire of USD 7,000 with confirmation number (04.10.2010), written acknowledgment of receipt (05.10.2010), no repayment. It is self-contained, fully documented, and requires no reconstruction of events across twenty years.

**4. Lead with s.320.** It needs no proof of the accused's state of mind in 2009 — only a debt admitted in writing (May 2024), a transfer (27.07.2026), and intent to defeat the creditor, inferable from the family transfer, absence of consideration, timing during litigation, and two decades of deferral on the strength of that very asset.

**5. Do not plead a figure above the admitted ₹40,00,000 until it is documented.** Quantum is not an element of any of these offences, and the complaint is framed on "not less than ₹40,00,000" with the balance expressly reserved. That preserves the larger claim without exposing the complainant to an attack on credibility, or to s.248 BNSS, for a sum he cannot presently prove. The larger figure is to be established from the accused's own accounts on production, and from interest on the basis he himself stated in 2010 — not from assertion.

**6. Get the earlier wills into the complaint.** A single unexecuted draft can be called a change of mind. A *series* of unexecuted drafts, each produced when repayment was pressed, is a device — and that is what makes s.318(2) unanswerable.

**7. s.63 BSA certificates are mandatory for every email.** Full headers, message-IDs, original mailbox rather than forwarded copies. Obtain before filing.

**8. s.223 proviso:** cognizance cannot be taken without giving the accused an opportunity of being heard. Expect the compensation defence — the valuation table in the draft will — to be run before process issues. Paragraph 13 is the answer: an unexecuted, revocable draft costs nothing and binds nothing.

**9. The litigation-intent facts are pleaded, the false-evidence charge is not — and that separation is deliberate.** Paragraphs 17 to 19 put the three pending suits and the accused's intent to damage them before this Court **as evidence of dishonesty**, which is permissible and materially strengthens ss.318, 320 and 321. The distinct offence of fabricating false evidence for use in a judicial proceeding (BNS ss.227–229) is **not** charged here: where the offence relates to a proceeding in a court, s.215(3) BNSS bars cognizance on a private complaint, and the route is an application to *that* court under s.379 r/w s.215. Confirm whether the instrument has been filed in O.S. 214/2025, then move the Munsiff's Court, Haripad separately. Charging it here would risk this complaint being returned.

**10. Civil suit to follow.** The May 2024 document is a written acknowledgement of the debt under s.18 Limitation Act, restarting limitation from that date. Recovery with interest is live to approximately May 2027. Add relief under **s.53 TPA** to set aside the 27.07.2026 instrument as a transfer to defeat creditors.
