APPLICATION UNDER ORDER XXVI RULE 10A R/W ORDER XI RULE 14, OR-
       DER XIII RULE 8 AND SECTION 151 OF THE CPC, 1908


The Plaintiff respectfully submits as follows:

1)  The Defendants seek to rely on documents recording critical decisions of
     the trustees on behalf of the Trust. The authenticity of the said documents
    must therefore be verified before they are acted upon.

2)   It is well settled that a Court may refer a document for scientific examina-
     tion — T. Nagappa v. Y.R. Muralidhar, (2008) 5 SCC 633.

PRAYER

In the above circumstances, it is most respectfully prayed that this Hon’ble
Court may be pleased to:

a)  direct the Defendants to produce, within seven (7) days, all originals on
   which decisions of the Trust are founded, and record that any document not
   so produced shall not be received at any later stage and shall be visited with
   an adverse inference against the Defendants.

b) retain the said originals in the custody of this Court, and not return them to
   the Defendants, until the examination is complete and the report is received.

c) refer the said originals, under sealed cover directly by this Court, to the Doc-
   uments Division, State Forensic Science Laboratory, Kerala Police Head-
   quarters, Thiruvananthapuram — for detailed forensic examination and re-
   port within six (6) weeks, comprising: (i) when the ink, writing and paper
   were actually made — by ink chromatography (TLC / HPTLC / GC-MS) and
   the paper’s optical brighteners; (ii) whether the entries and signatures were
  made on their stated dates or in one sitting — by ESDA and the sequence of
   strokes; and (iii) ink differences between entries, and any substituted leaves,
   under the Video Spectral Comparator.

Dated this the ______ day of July 2026.

Advocate
