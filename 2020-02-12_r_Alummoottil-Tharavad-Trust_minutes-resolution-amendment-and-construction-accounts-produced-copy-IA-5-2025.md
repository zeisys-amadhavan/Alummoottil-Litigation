**ALUMMOOTTIL THARAVAD TRUST**
Alummoottil House, Muttom P.O., Haripad, Alappuzha - 690 511
(Reg No: IV 2/2007 & 66/2022)

**Extract of Resolution taken on the Alummoottil Tharavad Trust meeting held on 12/02/2020**

1. "**Resolved** that the Managing trustee Sri Ramesh chandran channar is hereby authorized to take necessary action to amend the Alummoottil Tharavad Trust deed including preparation of the draft Amendment deed from time to time as deem fit'

2. "**Resolved** that Dr Sivadasan channar is hereby granted permission to carry out the renovation/rebuilding/construction of various buildings of Alummoottil tharavad complex by spending all the funds from his personal accounts for all the purposes in connection with the construction/renovation/rebuilding, including expenses such as Architects fee, PMC fee, special artisans work charges, consultation fee, legal fees *, property settlement charges, electricity charges, Govt tax, fees etc. till the completion* of the works." "**Resolved** that all other trustees agree that Dr Sivadasan channar is under no obligation to provide the details of any expenditure either to the trust or trustees or any other persons, whatso ever. Provided that by virtue of his spending money towards this, Dr Sivadasan Channar will not have any special rights in the trust except as provided in the trust deed amended from time to time. " **Resolved** that Dr Sivadasan channar is granted permission to select Contractors/Architects/Project Director, Manager, PMC management etc for the above."

3. "**Resolved** that the Managing trustee is hereby authorized to take necessary steps to do the settlement of properties belonging to all the five brothers viz. Dr Ravindran channar, Dr Sivadasan channar, Udayabhanu channar, Radhakrishnan channar and Rameshchandran channar to Alummoottil Tharavad trust."

4. "**Resolved** that the managing trustee shall maintain the books of accounts of Alummoottil Tharavad Trust as and when any amount is received and spent by the Trust."

Dr Ravindran
Udayabhanu M
Rameshchandran

## Transcription notes (observations only — no inference drawn)

1. **Letterhead anachronism:** the printed letterhead cites "Reg No: IV 2/2007 **& 66/2022**". Deed 66/IV/2022 was registered on 10-11-2022 — after the 12/02/2020 meeting this extract records. The extract as a physical document was therefore printed on or after the 2022 letterhead came into use. Stated as a fact about the stationery; nothing further inferred.
2. In the scan, the phrase ", *property settlement charges, electricity charges, Govt tax, fees etc. till the completion*" (resolution 2) appears in a visibly different, italic-style typeface from the surrounding text. Recorded as a visual observation of the photocopy only; whether it reflects the original document is a question for inspection of the original, not for this transcription.
3. Signature block lists four names — Dr Ravindran, Udayabhanu M, Rameshchandran, Sivadasan M. **Radhakrishnan does not appear.**
4. Resolution 4 places the duty to maintain books of accounts on the managing trustee "as and when any amount is received and spent by the Trust" — in the same document whose resolution 2 records that Dr Sivadasan is "under no obligation to provide the details of any expenditure". Both recorded as written.
