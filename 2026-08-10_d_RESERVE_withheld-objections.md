### Post-custody alteration — proved vs not proved

**Proved:** the 18.02.2026 extract and the 18.05.2026 minute-book original contain materially different tenure language; the court-held original is an independent comparison object.
**Not proved:** that the book was changed after 18.05.2026; that any leaf, ink entry or signature was added in custody; substitution of the court-held book; the chronological age of any disputed ink.
**Evidence needed before any post-custody claim:** the 18.05.2026 production memo and property/custody register entry · the court's intake scan or certified copy at lodgment · every later issue/return/inspection entry (recipient, date, time) · a new court-supervised high-resolution colour scan · only then, referral of the sealed original to a questioned-document laboratory (handwriting, ink, indentation, erasure, paper, sequence). **No destructive ink testing** until an examiner explains necessity, limitations and sampling, and the court authorises it.

### Legal handling — procedural cautions

1. **BSA 2023 does not apply to affidavits** presented to a court or officer (s.1(2)) — do not build an affidavit-falsehood application solely on BSA proof provisions; its primary/secondary-evidence and signature rules (ss.56–60, 65) remain relevant to the extracts, minute book, nomination and bank records when tendered as documentary evidence.
2. **BNSS s.215** generally requires the court's own written complaint (or an authorized/superior court's) for false-evidence offences committed in relation to its proceedings; **s.379** is the court's preliminary-inquiry and complaint mechanism.
3. **Threshold caution** (*Narendra Kumar Srivastava v. State of Bihar*, 2019; *Sciemed Overseas v. BOC India*, 2016 — predecessor numbering, same evidential caution): the court needs a reasonable foundation for a *deliberate, conscious falsehood on a matter of substance* and must find prosecution expedient — not act on every inaccuracy. **F1 is the best candidate** for that threshold (exact, material, visually demonstrable, tied to D2 as certifier); F2 and F3 corroborate reliability only.
