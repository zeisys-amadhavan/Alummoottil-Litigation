 OBJECTION OF THE PLAINTIFF TO I.A. NO. 7 OF 2026
FOR ADMISSION OF DOCUMENTS


1)  The Plaintiff objects to the Defendants' application dated 18.05.2026
     to receive their documents in evidence, admits none of them, and puts
    the Defendants to strict proof of each. The objections that follow go, in
     part, to admissibility in law to be decided before any document is
     received.

GENUINENESS AND BONA FIDES


2)  On 16.02.2026 the Defendants swore that these documents were "not
     available for production" (paragraph 13), and that "nothing has been
    concealed" (paragraph 14). On 18.05.2026 they produced the very
    same documents, in breach of that oath. The Defendants thus blow hot
    and cold. Records whose existence was denied on oath, surfacing only
   when their suppression was challenged, cannot be received as
    genuine without strict proof; their provenance is itself in issue and
    must be tested before reception.

3)  That affidavit was also the basis on which I.A. No. 5/2025 was closed
    on 04.03.2026, within the time the Hon'ble High Court had fixed for its
     disposal (order dated 15.01.2026 in O.P.(C) No. 3278/2025). That
     closure was thus an order obtained by suppression, and the Plaintiff
     reserves the right to challenge it before the appropriate forum.

LEASE DEED IS INADMISSIBLE


4)  The Lease Deed dated 27.10.2025 is a lease of immovable property,
    compulsorily registrable under Section 17 of the Registration Act,
    1908 and Section 107 of the Transfer of Property Act, 1882, and
     registrable only in the sub-district where the property lies — Cheppad,
    Alappuzha (Section 28).  It was instead registered at the Sub-
     Registrar's Office, Maradu, in the district of Ernakulam, an office with
    no territorial jurisdiction over the property, while being falsely
    described to this Hon'ble Court as "Marad, Alappuzha." to mislead the
     court that Registration happened in an office with jurisdiction, A void
     jurisdiction is no registration in law; the deed is therefore unregistered
     and, under Section 49 of the Registration Act, cannot be received to
    prove the lease (K.B. Saha & Sons (P) Ltd v. Development Consultant
     Ltd, (2008) 8 SCC 564). This objection goes to admissibility in law, not
     to weight or proof, and must therefore be determined now, before the
    document is received: the defect requires no evidence to establish, and
    cannot be cured at trial.

5)  The Plaintiff does not admit that the Lease Deed is duly and sufficiently
    stamped and puts the Defendants to proof. The adequacy of stamping
    must be examined before the deed is admitted: an instrument not duly
    stamped is inadmissible under Section 35 of the Kerala Stamp Act,
    1959, and once a document is admitted Section 36 bars any later
     challenge to its stamping (Javer Chand v. Pukhraj Surana, AIR 1961 SC
    1655); the Plaintiff therefore raises this objection now.

PRODUCTION IS SELECTIVE AND INCOMPLETE


6)  The production is selective. The lessee, Sreemoolavasam Wellness and
     Leisure Retreats Pvt. Ltd. (GSTIN 32ABQCS9416G1Z1), is an active
     GST-registered company  filing monthly returns from the  trust
    property itself at Alummoottil, Muttom — a running commercial
     venture. Such a venture generates records the Defendants have not
    produced: proof of stamp duty, the lessee's deposit, rent and its
     receipts, registration and incidental costs, and the matching entries in
    the Trust's accounts. They tendered the lease and a bank statement
    and withheld the rest. The Plaintiff reserves all recourse, including
    under Order XI Rule 21 CPC.

REMAINING DOCUMENTS ARE NOT ADMISSIBLE AS
TENDERED


7)  The statement of account is receivable, if at all, only on the certificate
    required by Section 2A of the Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 1891 (and,
     for any electronic record, Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya
    Adhiniyam, 2023) — not furnished. Tendered alone, it shows neither
    the deposit nor the rent such a venture must yield.

8)  The "nomination as beneficiary," the resolutions and the minutes book
    are the Defendants' own unproved records, put to strict proof of
    execution and genuineness.

ADDITIONAL OBJECTIONS RESERVED


9)  Without prejudice, the Plaintiff reserves, to be taken at the appropriate
     stage: (a) that the Lease Deed, tendered as a copy, is — if not a certified

    2023; (b) the legal effect of the documents — that Trust Deed No.
    2/IV/2007 gives no power to nominate a beneficiary (interest passing
    by lineal descent alone), that no resolution can confer, defeat or qualify
