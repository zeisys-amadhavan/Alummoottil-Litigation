## VERIFICATION NOTES — read before quoting in any pleading

1. **The Name field is blank.** Common for 1976 registrations, but it means this certificate proves *a male child born 02.06.1976 to Radhakrishnan and Sumathi Radhakrishnan* — the link to "Anoop R Madhavan" by name rests on corroborating identity documents (passport, school record, or a name-inclusion certificate obtainable from the Corporation of Trivandrum). If standing is attacked (risk R5), this is the seam an opponent would probe; consider obtaining the name-inclusion certificate proactively.
2. **Contemporaneous registration (28.06.1976)** — a strength; state it affirmatively if the certificate is ever challenged.
3. **Unclear handwriting to verify against the original:** father's permanent address ("Konnayil alkom"/"Konnavilakom"); the certificate issue date (20-?-1976); the issuing authority's signature.
4. The mother's name appears as **"Sumathi"** here and **"Sumathy"** elsewhere in the record (2018 emails: "Sumathy Radhakrishnan"; the will: "Sumathy Radhakrishnan alias Sumathy Chellappan") — a spelling variation, not a discrepancy, but note it once so it never surprises anyone.
