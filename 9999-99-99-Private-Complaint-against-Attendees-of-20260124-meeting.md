## I. JURISDICTION

1. The accused resides within the jurisdiction of this Hon'ble Court and made the representations complained of from within it, by electronic mail from mrk_alummood@yahoo.com.

## II. THE SCHEME

2. The accused is the complainant's father. Over twenty-six years he obtained money from the complainant in the name of Alummoottil and of his own business, upon the representation that the complainant would hold an equal, half share with his sister in the Alummoottil properties and in the accused's interest in the Alummoottil Tharavad Trust.

3. **Whenever the complainant sought repayment, the accused answered by holding out a testamentary promise — that the complainant's share would come to him under his will. He did this repeatedly, over years, sending the complainant successive drafts of wills.** Not one was ever executed. Each served its purpose, which was to defer repayment for a further period.

4. Having thus retained the complainant's money for close to two decades, the accused on 27.07.2026 executed — with full formality and without delay — an instrument naming his daughter Maliny R. Madhavan as the sole beneficiary of his line, excluding the complainant altogether.

5. The complainant is left with neither his money nor the share promised in its place. The accused retains both. His sister, who advanced nothing, takes everything.

## III. THE MONEY OBTAINED

6. **Not less than ₹40,00,000 — obtained not as a loan to the accused, but entrusted to him to be applied on the complainant's account.** Between 2006 and 2013 the accused's contracting business in the U.A.E., Stone Henge Contracting LLC, Dubai, suffered heavy losses. He sought and obtained from the complainant, over that period, sums aggregating **not less than ₹40,00,000** upon the 50-50 representation set out above. **By his letter of 26.09.2010, transmitted by email of the same date, the accused stated the basis on which he held that money in his own words:** *"I am managing this shortage of working capital by investing money as loan to the company **in your name**. So far Rs.12 lakhs is taken by me and an amount of Dhs. 111,130/- (Rs.14 lakhs, including interest up to Dec 2010)) cheque is already drawn in your name and sent through Ajayan (Investor & Engineer) to be kept with Sue."* The accused thus received the complainant's money not at his own free disposal but to be advanced to the company **in the complainant's name and for the complainant's account**, with interest accruing to the complainant, and a cheque for Dhs 111,130 was drawn in the complainant's name accordingly.

7. **The figure of ₹40,00,000 is the admitted minimum, not the total.** It is the amount which the accused himself chose to reduce to writing in May 2024. The complainant says the true aggregate is substantially higher, the payments having been made over some eight years, from abroad, through several channels, upon the accused's requests as and when made. The complainant pleads the admitted sum as a floor and expressly reserves the balance, the particulars of which are within the exclusive knowledge of the accused and are ascertainable from his bank accounts, from account No. 1052545600801 at Emirates NBD, Union Square Branch, Deira, Dubai, and from the books of Stone Henge Contracting LLC, Dubai — production of which is sought in the prayer below.

8. **Interest accrued to the complainant on the accused's own stated basis.** By the letter of 26.09.2010 the accused recorded that ₹12,00,000 then taken had become *"Dhs. 111,130/- (Rs.14 lakhs, including interest up to Dec 2010)"* — the interest being for the complainant's benefit, the advance standing in his name. The sum now due to the complainant is therefore the principal together with interest on that footing from the respective dates of advance, and not the bare figure of ₹40,00,000.

9. Neither the sums so advanced in the complainant's name, nor the said cheque, nor any interest, has ever been accounted for or made over to the complainant. `[State what became of the cheque of Dhs 111,130 said to have been sent through Ajayan and kept with Sumathy Radhakrishnan.]`

By email of 05.10.2010 the accused acknowledged: *"The money is received today."* The money was sought for a stated purpose, upon an express promise of repayment by a stated date, and has never been repaid.

## V. THE REPUDIATION

19. **The accused twice admitted the complainant's status in writing, and then repudiated it.** By his letter of 18.11.2024, transmitted by email of 06.12.2024, the accused wrote to the complainant: *"As my son and beneficiary of Alummoottil Tharavad Trust, you have access to the Trust properties and buildings"*; and further: *"You are a beneficiary of Alummoottil Tharavad Trust, just like your cousins Arun, Bimal, Aparna (Anju) etc. All the children in our next generation are equal. No special privilege to any one."* The sequence is therefore: **03.05.2024** — the complainant's share to be held "jointly and equally"; **18.11.2024** — the complainant is a beneficiary and all children of the next generation are equal; **27.07.2026** — the complainant's sister named sole beneficiary and the complainant excluded altogether.

20. When the accused wished a document to take effect, he executed it. The instrument of 27.07.2026 was executed in due form, in favour of a member of his own family, for no consideration moving to him, while the ₹40,00,000 he had admitted in writing stood wholly unpaid, and during the pendency of O.S. 214/2025.

21. **The instrument of 27.07.2026 is in direct contravention of the deed the accused himself executed.** The accused signed Trust Deed No. 2/IV/2007 as an Author and Trustee. That deed fixes the beneficiaries as **the descendants of Alummoottil Chellamma Channathy** — a class arising by birth and descent, which no trustee may enlarge, narrow or reassign. It confers on no trustee any power to designate a "sole beneficiary" of his line, or to exclude one descendant in favour of another. The accused, having himself created and signed that instrument nineteen years earlier, knew this. He nevertheless held the complainant's share out as the price of the complainant's money, and then executed a document purporting to take it away — an act beyond any power vested in him and contrary to the deed bearing his own signature.

22. **The instrument was executed to damage pending civil proceedings.** Three suits concerning the Alummoottil properties and the complainant's rights therein are pending before the Munsiff's Court, Haripad — **O.S. 84/2024** and **O.S. 214/2025**, in which the complainant is the plaintiff, and **O.S. 243/2024**, in which he is a defendant. In O.S. 214/2025 the complainant's status as a beneficiary is the very matter in issue.

23. The instrument of 27.07.2026 was created during the pendency of those suits, on that precise issue, and five weeks after the Managing Trustee of the Trust had sworn before that Court on 19.06.2026 that the complainant "was not nominated by any trustee" — the accused thereby supplying, after the event, the document that the said oath presupposed. The Trust's own minute of 06.02.2024 records that the accused had submitted no nomination for his line; it stood un-nominated for two and a half years, and a nomination was brought into existence only when it became useful against the complainant in litigation.

24. The complainant says that the instrument was executed with the dishonest intention of defeating his claims in the said suits, and that this circumstance establishes the dishonesty required by the sections set out below. The offences arising from the use of that instrument as evidence in those proceedings are reserved, and are being pursued before the Court concerned in accordance with law.

25. **The accused cannot explain why he took the complainant's money for Alummoottil across twenty-six years if the complainant was never to hold any share in it.** Either the complainant held the interest on the faith of which the money was taken — in which case the instrument of 27.07.2026 is a fraud upon him; or he never held it — in which case every rupee was obtained by deception.

## VI. THE PROGRESSIVE ALTERATION OF THE COMPLAINANT'S RECORDED STATUS

**A.** By Trust Deed No. 2/IV/2007, executed and signed by the accused, the beneficiaries are the descendants of Alummoottil Chellamma Channathy. The complainant, being such a descendant, stood recorded as a beneficiary from that date.

**B.** On 03.05.2024 the accused confirmed in writing that his share in the Alummoottil Tharavad Trust would pass to the complainant and his sister *"jointly and equally."*

**C.** On 18.11.2024 the accused confirmed in writing, twice, that the complainant is *"my son and beneficiary of Alummoottil Tharavad Trust,"* and that he is *"a beneficiary of Alummoottil Tharavad Trust, just like your cousins Arun, Bimal, Aparna (Anju) etc. All the children in our next generation are equal. No special privilege to any one."*

**D.** On 24.01.2026 the minutes of the Trust recorded the complainant as *"the beneficiary son of Trustee Radhakrishnan Channar."* **The word "beneficiary" has been struck through by pen in that record.** The alteration is neither countersigned nor dated nor initialled by any person. The same page carries an insertion made by caret. The accused is a signatory to those minutes.

**E.** On 27.07.2026 the accused executed a document purporting to name his daughter as the sole beneficiary of his line, excluding the complainant altogether.

**F.** The progression is therefore: a status conferred by the registered deed the accused himself signed; twice reaffirmed by him in writing in 2024; struck out by an uncountersigned pen alteration in a record he signed in January 2026; and finally reassigned to his daughter by an instrument executed in July 2026 during the pendency of three suits. The complainant says that this was a deliberate and progressive falsification of his recorded status, carried out to defeat his claim to his money and to his rights in the Trust, and that each step was taken with knowledge that the accused possessed no power to alter a class fixed by descent under the deed of 2007.

## VII. OFFENCES

26. **s.318(4)** — the accused dishonestly induced the complainant to deliver ₹40,00,000 and further sums upon a representation of an equal share which he did not intend to honour.

27. **s.318(2)** — by successive unexecuted draft wills, and by repeating the 50-50 assurance whenever repayment was sought, the accused intentionally induced the complainant to omit to enforce recovery of his money, causing him damage. Each occasion is a distinct act of deception.

28. **s.320** — being indebted in a sum admitted in his own writing, the accused dishonestly and fraudulently dealt with the very property held out as the means of satisfying that debt, by the instrument of 27.07.2026, executed during the pendency of the said suits and contrary to the deed he had himself signed, with intent to prevent that property being available for payment of that debt.

29. **s.321** — the accused thereby fraudulently prevented a demand due to the complainant from being made available according to law for payment of that debt.

**s.316(5) BNS — criminal breach of trust by an agent.** By his letter of 26.09.2010 the accused acknowledged that he received and held the complainant's money in the capacity of his agent — to be advanced to Stone Henge Contracting LLC **in the complainant's name**, with interest accruing to the complainant, and with a cheque for Dhs 111,130 drawn in the complainant's name. Being so entrusted in the capacity of agent, and having dominion over that money, the accused dishonestly used and disposed of it otherwise than in accordance with that entrustment, and has to this day rendered no account of the money, of the advance, of the interest, or of the instrument drawn in the complainant's name. In the alternative, the offence falls under s.316(2).
