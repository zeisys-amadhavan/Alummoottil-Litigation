**Extract of Resolution taken on the Alummoottil Tharavad Trust meeting held on 21-08-2025**

1 "Resolved to authorize the Managing Trustee Sri Ramesh chandran Channar to find a suitable operator for leasing out the Alummoottil Meda, Nalukettu, Pathayapura and /or any or all of the adjacent buildings in full or part, to be run as a Destination/Resort/Ayurvedic Resort or any other similar establishments as the operator may decide. He is also hereby authorized to negotiate with at least 3 such operators in the field and to select any one among them including specifying the period of lease, lease rent, profit share and all other terms and conditions therewith, as he may deem fit, in the best interest of the trust."

2 " Resolved to authorize the Managing Trustee Sri M Ramesh chandran Channar to enter into and sign, on behalf of the trust, any agreement, MOA, or any document pertaining to lease, operation and maintenance of the Destination/Resort/Ayurvedic Resort or any other similar establishment with any individual , firm, or body corporate and get it registered.

3 " Resoled to authorize the Managing Trustee Sri Ramesh chandran Channar to get all such documents vetted by a legal professional before he signs such documents on behalf of the Trust."
