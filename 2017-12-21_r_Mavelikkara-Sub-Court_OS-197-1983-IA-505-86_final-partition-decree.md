## Body of the decree

This is an application filed by the Plaintiff/Petitioner in the case for passing Final Decree on the basis of preliminary decree dated **28.06.1985** in O.S. 197/1983. The preliminary decree was passed as follows:

> "The plaint scheduled property will be divided into **6 equal shares** and to allot **5/6 shares** to the plaintiffs 1 to 3 and first and second defendants jointly and this court further directed the commissioner to allot **1/6 share** to the supplementary 4, 5 and 6, the legal representatives of the deceased second defendant."

In the Final Decree application respondents 1 to 5 entered appearance.

This court had deputed **Adv. Sri. S. Mohanan, Commissioner**, who measured out the properties with the assistance of a surveyor and submitted a report and plan. The said plan and report were **set aside** and the commissioner was directed to effect partition as per the direction in **IA 1877/2010** in O.S. 197/83. The commissioner who effected the partition as per the direction of the court submitted a report and plan.

The **expert commissioner** appointed by the court submitted a report regarding the **valuation of the buildings** situated in the preliminary-decree scheduled land. The supplemental 5th respondent filed a petition for setting aside the commissioner's report and plan; the application was **dismissed vide order dated 13.12.2017 in IA 270/17**. No further steps were taken. The commissioner's report and plan were marked as **Ext. C1 and C1(a)**; the expert commissioner's report was marked as **Ext. C2**.

> **"The respondents did not adduce any evidence regarding the availability of the gold ornaments."**

The petition was finally heard on **20.12.2017**, and on **21.12.2017** the final decree was passed as follows:

**(1)** The plot marked as **KLNOPQRCSTUV** on Ext. C1(a) plan is staked out for the formation of a **common pathway** to the plots described 'HIJKLHA1B1C1D1E1F1G1H1J1QPONLM1'. *(plot-letter string as printed; scan partly unclear)*

**(2)** The plot marked as **HIJKLM** on Ext. C1(a) plan, comprised in **resurvey No. 219/29**, having an extent of **14.524 cents (5.88 Ares)**, is allotted to the share of the **supplemental respondents 4 to 7** (LRs of the deceased 2nd defendant), vide the supplementary preliminary decree dt. **31.10.1992** as per IA 1944/90 & 1091/92.

**(3)** The plot marked as **HA1B1C1D1E1F1G1H1J1QPONLM1** on Ext. C1(a) plan, comprised in **resurvey No. 219/11**, having an extent of **72.696 cents (29.42 Ares)**, is allotted to the share of the **plaintiffs and the 1st and 3rd defendants**.

The parties being members of a family, they are liable to pay stamp duty u/s **42(1) of the Kerala Stamp Act** (the extent of the land does not exceed 5 acres). The parties are directed to deposit **Rs. 1,000/-** towards non-judicial stamp paper for engrossing the Final Decree within 30 days.

**Exhibits C1, C1(a) & C2 shall form part of the final decree.** The final decree engrossed on stamp paper shall be sent to the **Sub Registrar** within whose jurisdiction the properties scheduled in this case are situated, under **Rule 237(2) of the Civil Rules of Practice**. **The parties shall take steps for registration of the Instrument of partition.**

## Observations for the file (analysis, not part of the decree)

1. **29.42 Ares in Re.Sy. 219/11 — the decree plot equals the lease's headline extent.** The 21.12.2017 final decree allots 72.696 cents (29.42 Ares) in Re.Sy. 219/11 to the plaintiffs (Sivadas, Udayabhanu, Ramesh Chandran) and the 1st and 3rd defendants (Dr. Raveendran, Radhakrishnan) — i.e., the five brothers. The Lease Deed 3237/2025 recites the demised land as 29.42 Ares (21.81 in 219/11 + fractions in 219/29, 219/19, 219/28-1, 219/27-1). The identical 29.42 figure with differing survey composition should be reconciled against Ext. C1(a) and the settlement deeds before use.
2. **D2 was a petitioner; Radhakrishnan (plaintiff's father) a respondent-allottee.** The five-brother co-ownership crystallized by this decree is the root of title recited (via Settlement Deeds 299/2024, 504/2025) in the impugned lease.
3. **Gold ornaments were in issue in the partition** — "respondents did not adduce any evidence regarding the availability of the gold ornaments" — a 2017 judicial record that family gold/heirlooms were a live subject, relevant background to I.A. 9/2025 (heirloom custody).
4. **219/29 (5.88 Ares) went to the Saradamani/Gopinathan line** — yet the lease demises 0.65 Are in 219/29. Whether that fraction reached the Trust via the 2021–22 gift deeds (1481/1482) or otherwise must be traced; if not, the lease may demise land outside the Trust's title.
5. **Registration direction:** the decree was ordered to be sent to the Sub Registrar and the parties directed to register the instrument of partition. Whether registration was completed is **[TO CONFIRM]** (SRO Cheppad index / EC).
6. The decree date **21.12.2017** is the natural cut-line for "construction since the family's rights crystallized" queries (building permits, alterations) in RTI work.
